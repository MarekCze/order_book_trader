## Key findings (see `runs/tuning-log.md`, `runs/*-results.md`)

- **Binding constraints (why setups are silent):** S1 dies at **A4** (structural — stall
  volume at the level is a *fraction* of baseline, never 3×; A5/A6 also block; opening A4 down
  to ×0.2 still yields ~0 trades). S4 dies at **D2** (threshold-calibration — fires at ~×0.6 of
  `DepthDeclineFraction`/`PullRatioMin`, but on a cliff: 0→50k candidates). S2 throttled by
  B3/B5. **S5 is the only setup that trades** by default.
- **"Many entrances" is architecturally gated, not threshold-gated:** permissive run = 70,133
  candidates but only 48 trades — **95% blocked `PositionOpen`** (the hard-coded
  one-position-at-a-time rule). Loosening thresholds also **destroys entry quality** (hit rate
  31%→8%) — the strict thresholds are quality filters.
- **Why so many losses → upside-down R:R:** wins capped at ≈+0.5R (T1 50% @1R, runner parks at
  breakeven behind an **unreachable 3R T2** — hit 0/16), losses run −1R → needs ~79% hit rate.
- **Exit-geometry fix (config-only):** **P2 = take 100% at the 1R target** beats baseline on the
  same trades — avg win $173.5→$486 (×2.8), expectancy −$385→−$287 (~25%), payoff 0.27→0.76,
  lowest maxDD. BUT still net-negative: needs ~57% hit rate vs actual 31% → **entry quality is
  the binding ceiling.** **Trailing-stop code judged NOT worth building** (only 6% of trades
  reach 3R). User chose to **stop here** (P2 not adopted as default yet).
- **Inverting trade direction makes it WORSE, not better** (`runs/inverted-direction-results.md`):
  35 trades, 4W/31L, net **−$17,219** vs baseline −$6,162; hit rate 31%→11%. **Direction is not
  the problem — fading is the correct side.** Buying highs / selling lows gets caught by the same
  mean-reversion the fades target. Confirms (3rd time) the losses are **entry quality + R:R/costs,
  not the side.**
